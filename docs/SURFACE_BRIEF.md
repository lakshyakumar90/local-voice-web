# Homepage surface brief

Mode: Persuade, within the established Local Voice identity. Existing PRODUCT.md and the previously approved Android asset/website handoff supply product truth and visual authority; this is implementation of that direction, not a new brand tournament. Next.js and Motion are explicit user requirements.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Speech becomes useful writing on the user's own phone. Visitors understand the app and can download localvoice.apk without navigating a sales funnel.
OWN-WORLD: Inherit porcelain, forest, teal and a single citron accent; Manrope; rounded waveform strokes; original glass-wave art. Vary broad story panels, table and quiet text rather than identical feature tiles.
STORY: Understand offline dictation, see a labeled formatting example, learn about privacy/models, download and follow setup. Creator profiles and factual privacy/terms stay findable.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Large left-aligned two-line thought-to-writing headline, Android explanation and direct download; dimensional original art on the right at desktop, stacked below on mobile. Small release details remain readable.
FORM: Code-led implementation of the established, previously approved asset-handoff composition; no new visual world or concept seed. Reference: C:/Lakshya/wispr/assets/brand/previews/website-layout-study.png. Requested vibebrand.design cannot resolve through both Agent Reach/Jina and Firecrawl; no unsupported claim of reviewing it.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: user-triggered language-aware formatting example with Motion state transitions; one GSAP scroll-linked privacy illustration on desktop only. Native scrolling and reduced-motion support. No microphone access requested by the website.

Known first-review findings: hero art overflows at 1000px CSS viewport; standalone tiny-link/tab targets should be enlarged. Desktop headline can wrap beyond the intended two lines at some widths. Batch these with independent reviewer findings before one confirmation pass.
